## ⚠️ Check these before the first day
- [ ] **Route 158 landslide (Takayama ↔ Hirayu).** A landslide closed ~2.4 km of Rte 158 at Shioya (Nyukawa-cho). As of the last update I found (Jul 14) the reopening was "undetermined"; passenger cars could detour via Takayama city roads (~+10 min). Check [Okuhida tourism topics](https://www.okuhida.or.jp/en/topics) the morning of Nov 18.
- [ ] **Nov 15 pickup is 14:00.** Sunset at Kawaguchiko is 16:39, so with a 14:00 pickup you reach Oshino Hakkai at dusk at best. Either ask Toyota Shinjuku (03-3341-2603) to move pickup earlier, or drop the optional stops (see Nov 15).
- [ ] **Nikko → Tokyo train** has no time or seat yet ([[Legs/02-Nikko-to-Tokyo]]); the pickup depends on it.
- [ ] **Winter tires** are not on the booking. Okuhida advises studless tires or chains in winter; confirm at pickup (4WD is booked).
- [ ] **Return shop:** the booking says Kanazawa **West** Exit (Hirooka). Switch to **East** Exit (Porte Kanazawa 1F), the closer one for Kanazawa Station and the hotel.
- [ ] **Parking** at Hotel Regina, Hotel Buena Vista and Hotel around TAKAYAMA (2 nights): cost and height limit unknown.

**Rules of thumb**
- Weekdays Tue-Thu are the quietest on the Chuo Expressway; Saturday outbound (06:00-11:00) and Sunday inbound (15:00-20:00) are the worst (source: [banzokubiology.com](https://banzokubiology.com/chuo-expressway-youbi-konzatsu/), general patterns, not live data).
- Mountain legs only between ~09:30 and ~15:30. Dusk is early (sunset 16:36-16:45); do not plan any mountain driving after ~16:00.
- Fill the tank before Rte 158 (Nov 17-18 evening or the Niimura station below) and before Shirakawa-go. Stations are scarce in the mountains.
- Check live traffic on Google Maps / Waze the morning of each leg; tolls ~¥8,000-12,000 for the whole route (rough estimate in [[Transport]], not verified), use the ETC card.

## 🍽️ One-night / two-night town cheat sheet

**Fujikawaguchiko (2 nights)** · must: Fuji at dawn from Oishi Park, hoto noodles, Momiji Kairo at night · skip: Yamanaka and Saruhashi unless you pick up early · tip: the Autumn Leaves Festival runs Nov 7-29, so the evening spots are busy on Nov 15-16.

**Matsumoto (1 night)** · must: the castle (before 16:30), Nakamachi and Nawate streets, one soba or oyaki · eat: Kobayashi Soba is the famous one but is lunch-only on Tuesday and closed Wed/Thu; cohiludo has the best-reviewed oyaki · tip: dinner choices are thin on a Tuesday, book Baniku Baru or ask the hotel.

**Takayama (2 nights)** · must: Sanmachi Suji, morning market, Jinya, Hida beef (sushi at lunch, grilled at dinner) · eat: ramen at Kamon or Menya Shirakawa · tip: this town closes early (shops ~17:00, many restaurants shut 14:00-17:00), plan lunch before 14:00.

**Shirakawa-go (1.5 hours)** · must: the village walk over the bridge, the Shiroyama viewpoint if you have 45 min · tip: Friday morning before the buses; parking lot fills later in the day.
