# 🚗 Car Trip: Tokyo → Kanazawa

> [!info] At a glance
> **Nov 15 → Nov 20, 2026.** Train Nikko → Tokyo, **pick up the rental in Tokyo**, drive Fujikawaguchiko → Matsumoto → Takayama, then drop the car in **Kanazawa** (one-way rental).

## 🗓️ Day-by-day

### Nov 15: Tokyo → Fujikawaguchiko
- Tokyo (train back from Nikko, pick up car)
- Lake Yamanaka
- Oshino Hakkai
- Fujikawaguchiko

> [!warning] Tight day
> Sunset is around 4:40pm. Aim to be driving by ~1pm. If running late, **drop Lake Yamanaka** and keep Oshino Hakkai.

### Nov 16: Fuji loop (local)
- Fujikawaguchiko
- Lake Kawaguchi
- Oishi Park
- Mt. Tenjo
- Saiko

> [!tip]
> Do **Mt. Tenjo first**, since Fuji is usually clearest in the morning. Then Oishi Park, then Saiko. Consider adding Saiko Iyashi no Sato (thatched-roof village).

### Nov 17: Fujikawaguchiko → Matsumoto
- Kiso Valley / Route 19
- Narai-juku
- Matsumoto

> [!warning] Longest driving day
> Roughly 4.5-5h plus stops. Leave by ~8:30am. Keep it to **Narai-juku** and skip Magome/Tsumago, which would add hours.

### Nov 18: Matsumoto → Takayama
- Route 158
- Northern Alps scenic stops
- Hirayu Great Falls
- Takayama

> [!warning] Mountain road
> Snow/ice possible. Check Route 158 conditions that morning and fill the tank beforehand. **Kamikochi is likely closed** (typically closes mid-November).

### Nov 19: Takayama (no car)
- Takayama Old Town
- Miyagawa Morning Market
- Takayama Jinya
- Hida Furukawa *(optional)*

### Nov 20: Takayama → Kanazawa
- Shirakawa-go
- Shirakawa-go Viewpoint
- Kanazawa
- **Return rental car**

> [!tip]
> Shirakawa-go is very crowded. Use the public lot and arrive by ~9am if possible. Return the car in Kanazawa by late afternoon.
