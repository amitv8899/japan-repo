# Tokyo (Arrival) — Food

> [!abstract] Back-links

> First landing. Shinjuku base at Keio Plaza. Beat jet lag with an easy first day, ramp up after.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) — tiny yakitori alleys 3 min from Keio Plaza; smoky, atmospheric
- [ ] Tsunahachi Shinjuku — classic tempura since 1923
- [ ] Ichiran / Ichiran Shinjuku — solo-booth tonkotsu ramen, good jet-lag comfort food
- [ ] Standing sushi at Uogashi Nihonichi (Shinjuku) — cheap, fast, fresh
- [ ] Must-try: fresh uni, otoro, wagyu, conveyor-belt sushi, a proper konbini egg-salad sando
- [ ] Depachika food hall at Isetan Shinjuku — bento, wagashi, pastries for the room
