> First landing. Shinjuku base at Keio Plaza. Beat jet lag with an easy first day, ramp up after.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Hotel: Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, 2-2-1 Nishishinjuku — 5 min walk from Shinjuku Station west exit
- [ ] From Narita: N'EX to Shinjuku (~90 min) or Airport Limousine bus to hotel door
- [ ] From Haneda: Airport Limousine bus direct to Keio Plaza (~45 min) — easiest with luggage
- [ ] Get Suica/PASMO (or Welcome Suica) or add Suica to phone wallet on arrival
- [ ] Set up eSIM before landing; grab pocket wifi only if multiple devices
- [ ] Luggage forward (takuhaibin) from hotel to later stay if skipping a leg
- [ ] Emergency: Police 110, Fire/Ambulance 119
