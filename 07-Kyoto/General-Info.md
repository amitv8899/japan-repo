## Suggestions
- [ ] Hotel: Good Nature Hotel Kyoto, Kawaramachi/Shijo — central, walkable to Nishiki & Gion
- [ ] From Kanazawa: Thunderbird ltd exp to Tsuruga + Hokuriku Shinkansen, or direct rail (~2–2.5h)
- [ ] Buses crowded in foliage season — prefer subway/train + walking; get ICOCA/Suica
- [ ] Consider early-morning temple runs, midday market/shopping, evening illuminations
- [ ] 6 nights = good base for Nara/Osaka day hits
- [ ] Forward luggage between Kyoto and later stays to travel light on day trips
