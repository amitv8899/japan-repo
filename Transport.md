## 🚗 Rental car — Tokyo → Kanazawa

> [!important] The driving segment
> Pick up in Tokyo after returning from Nikko; drive the scenic inland route; drop off at Kanazawa. This is **one-way rental** (different pickup/return city) — expect a drop-off fee.

### Drive legs

| Leg | From → To | Distance | Drive time | Route / notes |
|-----|-----------|:--------:|:----------:|---------------|
| A | Tokyo → Fujikawaguchiko | ~110 km | ~2h | Chuo Expressway; traffic leaving Tokyo — start early |
| B | Fujikawaguchiko → Matsumoto | ~130 km | ~2.5h | Via Chuo Expwy/Rte 20; passes Suwa lake area |
| C | Matsumoto → Takayama | ~110 km | ~2–2.5h | **Rte 158 / Abo Pass (tunnel)** — mountain, near Kamikochi; ⚠️ winter conditions |
| D | Takayama → Kanazawa | ~150 km | ~2h | Tokai-Hokuriku Expwy; **optional detour: Shirakawa-go** (~50 min) |

> [!warning] Winter driving — late November
> Legs C (Abo Pass) and the higher sections can have **snow/ice by late Nov**. Insist on **studless winter tires** (many rentals switch over Nov–Mar — confirm), carry/rent **chains**, and check pass/road status (some alpine roads close for winter). Allow extra time; avoid night mountain driving.

### Driving tips
- Japan drives on the **left**; give way, speed limits low (expwy ~80–100)
- Navigate by **map code** or phone number in the car GPS (get map codes for each stop/ryokan)
- **ETC lane** = don't stop at tollgates (with ETC card); otherwise use "一般/General" lane, pay cash/card
- Confirm **parking** at each ryokan/hotel (Fuji, Matsumoto, Takayama, Kanazawa) + overnight cost
- Fuel: **self / full-service** stands; "regular" (レギュラー) for most rentals
- Keep the car light — **forward big luggage** by takuhaibin where possible
