## Suggestions
- [ ] Dinner, no horse meat (Amit doesn't eat it): Takefudo Matsumoto Nakamachi (weekday 17:30-21:00, L.O. 20:00), Kura no Mukou (soba izakaya near the station), Kawasho (sanzoku-yaki, in Asama Onsen, needs bus/taxi) — all unverified, confirm Tuesday hours and menu
- [ ] Soba — Shinshu soba is top-tier; try Kobayashi or Nawate-dori spots
- [ ] Zumar/Matsumoto craft coffee scene
- [ ] Oyaki (stuffed dumplings) as a snack
- [ ] Sanzoku-yaki — crispy garlic-soy fried chicken cutlet, local specialty near the castle
- [ ] Must-try: Shinshu soba, wasabi (from nearby Azumino), local apples, sanzoku-yaki
