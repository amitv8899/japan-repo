# Matsumoto — Food

> [!abstract] Back-links
> Folder: [[04-Matsumoto]] · Itinerary: [[Japan Trip]]

> [!tip] Food — restaurants, cafés, must-try dishes
> One night. Black 'Crow' castle + craft/coffee town in the Alps. Compact, walkable.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Sinchan / local izakaya for basashi (horse sashimi) — Nagano specialty
- [ ] Soba — Shinshu soba is top-tier; try Kobayashi or Nawate-dori spots
- [ ] Zumar/Matsumoto craft coffee scene
- [ ] Oyaki (stuffed dumplings) as a snack
- [ ] Sanzoku-yaki — crispy garlic-soy fried chicken cutlet, local specialty near the castle
- [ ] Must-try: Shinshu soba, basashi, wasabi (from nearby Azumino), local apples, sanzoku-yaki
