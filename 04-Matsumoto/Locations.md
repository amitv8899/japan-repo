# Matsumoto — Locations

> [!abstract] Back-links
> Folder: [[04-Matsumoto]] · Itinerary: [[Japan Trip]]

> [!tip] Locations — sights, temples, neighborhoods, day trips
> One night. Black 'Crow' castle + craft/coffee town in the Alps. Compact, walkable.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Matsumoto Castle — original 16th-c keep, black facade + Alps backdrop; mid-Nov the black exterior contrasts with yellow ginkgo + red maples along the moat
- [ ] Nawate-dori 'frog street' + Nakamachi white-wall storehouse street — walk over from the castle for lunch (soba or sanzoku-yaki)
- [ ] Matsumoto City Museum of Art (Yayoi Kusama hometown)
- [ ] Metoba River walk
- [ ] Optional Daio Wasabi Farm (Azumino) if time
