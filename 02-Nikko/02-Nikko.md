Trip Strategy: Hands-Free & Scenic

- **Duration:** 2 Nights (The perfect pace to experience both history and nature without rushing).
- **Transit Setup:** Coming from Tokyo and returning to Tokyo, where you will pick up a rental car to drive to Mount Fuji.
- **Luggage Strategy:** You are **forwarding your heavy bags** ahead from Tokyo to your Nikko hotel. You will travel to Nikko light, using only a daypack.
- **Transportation Pass:** You will use the **NIKKO PASS All Area** (purchased in Tokyo), which covers your round-trip train and gives you unlimited free rides on the mountain buses.

---

🗺️ Master 2-Night Itinerary

⛩️ Day 1: The Spiritual Valley & Mountain Arrival

- **Morning:** Take the Tobu Limited Express train from Tokyo (Asakusa Station) to Tobu-Nikko Station.
- **Midday:** Walk straight out of the station to **Bus Stop 2**. Take the bus to the World Heritage area.
- **Afternoon Walking Tour (3-4 Hours):** Walk up the mountain forest paths to see all four major historical sites in geographical order:
    1. **Shinkyo Bridge**: The iconic red sacred gateway.
    2. **Rinnoji Temple**: Housing three massive gold Buddhist statues.
    3. **Toshogu Shrine**: The lavish gold-leaf masterpiece with the famous "Three Wise Monkeys" carving.
    4. **Taiyuin Mausoleum**: The peaceful, quiet sanctuary surrounded by towering cedars.
- **Late Afternoon:** Catch the **Line Y Tobu Bus** directly from the _Taiyuin-byo-mae_ bus stop at the exit of the shrine complex. Ride it up the winding Irohazaka Slope to your mountain hotel.
- **Evening:** Check into your resort, unpack your forwarded luggage, and relax in the volcanic hot spring (_onsen_).

🥾 Day 2: Deep Mountain Nature & Waterfalls

- **Morning:** Visit **Kegon Falls**, taking the elevator down into the canyon gorge to see the 97-meter-tall waterfall up close.
- **Midday:** Take a short bus ride further up the mountain to the **Senjogahara Marshland** for a scenic, flat walk along the wooden boardwalk trails.
- **Afternoon:** End your hike at the roaring **Yutaki Falls** and enjoy a local lunch at the food stalls at the base.
- **Evening:** Return to your lakeside resort for another relaxing onsen soak and a traditional multi-course dinner.

🚗 Day 3: Return to Tokyo & Mount Fuji Road Trip

- **Morning:** Take the Tobu Bus down the mountain back to Tobu-Nikko Station.
- **Midday:** Board the Limited Express train back to Tokyo.
- **Afternoon:** Pick up your rental car in Tokyo and begin your 2-hour drive to the Mount Fuji region.
