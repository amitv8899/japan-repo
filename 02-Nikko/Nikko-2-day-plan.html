Bags, food and money

  • Backpacks: coin lockers outside the gate and at the tourist information center, from about ¥400 per day in 100-yen coins; fit not confirmed. The courier counter sends bags to the Chuzenji area (accepts until 14:00, delivers by 17:00, fee by size), whether it serves Kanaya verify.
  • Dinner at the hotel: Mizunara 18:00–20:00 (last order); breakfast 7:30–9:30. Price, reservation and whether dinner is in your plan verify. Dress: no tank tops, shorts, flip-flops or room yukata.
  • Lunch near the lake: Yukon coffee house, 11:30–15:30 (last order 15:00), about 4 minutes on foot from the hotel.
  • No restaurants open at night near the hotel and no convenience stores (vending machines only). Buy water and snacks in town today.
  • Cash: Fri about ¥9,600 for two excluding dinner; Sat about ¥6,000–9,000 estimate. No ATM verified near the hotel.
  • Sunday: the hotel shuttle down leaves 8:50, too late for the 08:58 train. Ask the hotel at check-in about a taxi (~30 min) or an early bus. Rain forecast. Pack before bed on Saturday.
