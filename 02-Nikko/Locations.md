# Nikko — Locations

> [!abstract] Back-links
> Folder: [[02-Nikko]] · Itinerary: [[Japan Trip]]

> [!tip] Locations — sights, temples, neighborhoods, day trips
> Mountain shrines + waterfalls. Late Nov the high Chuzenji area may be cold/early-winter; town shrines still gorgeous.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Toshogu Shrine — ornate UNESCO complex (Tokugawa Ieyasu mausoleum)
- [ ] Rinno-ji temple + Shinkyo sacred bridge
- [ ] Kegon Falls — 97m, dramatic (elevator to base)
- [ ] Lake Chuzenji + Chuzenji Onsen
- [ ] Irohazaka winding road (foliage often done by late Nov up high)
- [ ] Nikko Futarasan Shrine — mountain-top Shinto shrine, 8th-century origins
- [ ] ~~Akechidaira Ropeway~~ — CLOSED for renewal from Jan 16, 2026 until ~Sept 2027; not available on this trip
- [ ] Kanmangafuchi Abyss — row of Jizo statues, quiet walk
- [ ] Shoyoen Garden (Rinnoji) — pond maples, "approaching peak" on Oct 22 (japan-guide); hours/fee unverified
- [ ] Senjogahara boardwalk + Ryuzu Falls — peak colour on Oct 22; likely past by mid-Nov, check
- [ ] Yudaki Falls — end of Senjogahara walk, bus back toward Chuzenji
- [ ] Chuzenji-ji (Tachiki Kannon) + Futarasan Shrine Chuzenji-ko — lakeside near Chuzenji Onsen, Nov 14 morning after Kegon; hours/fees unverified
- [ ] Italian Embassy Villa Memorial Park — lakeside, quiet, Nov 14 if time; hours unverified (may be reduced or closed in late autumn)
- [ ] British Embassy Villa Memorial Park (Chuzenji lakeside) — Tobu: 9:00-16:00 from Nov 11-30, closed Mondays in Nov (open Sat Nov 14); fee unverified. Alternative to the Italian Villa, whose Nov hours are unverified
- [ ] Italian + British Embassy Villa Memorial Parks (japan-guide): 9:00-16:00 Nov 11-30, ¥300 each or ¥450 combined, closed Dec-Mar; British villa has a tea cafe (hours not stated)
- [ ] Futarasan Chuzenji-ko Shrine: 9:00-16:00 Nov-Mar, free (¥300 for small paid area)
- [ ] Nikko Natural Science Museum, next to Chuzenji Onsen bus terminal: 10:00-16:00 mid Nov-Mar, ¥510 — indoor option
- [ ] Lake Chuzenji sightseeing boat (mid Apr-Nov): ¥1,850 full loop, 1 hour; latest sailing in Nov unverified
