# Fujikawaguchiko — Locations

> [!abstract] Back-links
> Folder: [[03-Fujikawaguchiko]] · Itinerary: [[Japan Trip]]

> [!tip] Locations — sights, temples, neighborhoods, day trips
> Lakeside Fuji views. Clear crisp Nov mornings = best chance of a clean Mt Fuji. Onsen + red autumn leaves.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Chureito Pagoda (Arakura Sengen) — the iconic pagoda+Fuji shot, ~400 steps
- [ ] Lake Kawaguchiko north shore — classic reflected Fuji
- [ ] Momiji Kairo (Maple Corridor), north shore of Lake Kawaguchi — leaf-covered tunnel, peak late Oct–mid Nov; from Kawaguchiko Stn take Kawaguchiko Sightseeing Bus Red Line to Kubota Itchiku Art Museum, then walk ~5 min
- [ ] Oishi Park — lavender/kochia beds + Fuji
- [ ] Kachi Kachi Ropeway (Mt Tenjo) for panorama
- [ ] Oshino Hakkai — spring-water ponds village
- [ ] Momiji Kaido (Kawaguchiko Museum street), ~1.2 km maple-lined road, festival spot — hours/parking unverified
- [ ] Funatsu-hama / Hayashisaku Park (Funatsu lakeside, same district as Hotel Regina) — festival spot, short walk for dusk if the rain stops; distance unverified
- [ ] Yagisaki Park + Okukawaguchiko Sakura no Sato Park — festival foliage spots on the north shore; hours unverified
- [ ] Shoji-ko Tebanahama (Lake Shoji) — festival spot, 'peak now' per Nov 13 release; drive time from Hotel Regina unverified
- [ ] Itchiku Kubota Art Museum — indoor rain backup next to Momiji Kairo; hours/closed days/fee unverified (check before going)
- [ ] Oishi Tsumugi Traditional Craft Hall (大石紬伝統工芸館), Oishi 1438-1, tel 0555-76-7901 — 9:00-16:00, closed Tue (2021 info); hand-weaving ¥7,000 (reservation, ~90 min), plant handkerchief dyeing ¥1,500
- [ ] Saiko Iyashi-no-Sato Nenba — thatched village, 9:00-17:00 in Nov, no closing days, adults ¥500
