# Kanazawa — Food

> [!abstract] Back-links
> Folder: [[06-Kanazawa]] · Itinerary: [[Japan Trip]]

> [!tip] Food — restaurants, cafés, must-try dishes
> Three nights. Gold leaf, geisha districts, one of Japan's top gardens, superb seafood.

## Suggestions
- [ ] Omicho Market — kaisendon (seafood bowls), grilled crab, sushi; go for lunch
- [ ] Kanazawa is famous for gold-leaf soft-serve ice cream
- [ ] Jibuni (duck/wheat-gluten stew) — local specialty
- [ ] Sushi: Ippei / Mori Mori Sushi (Kanazawa Station) for value
- [ ] Must-try: snow crab (zuwaigani — Nov season opens!), nodoguro, gold-leaf treats
- [ ] Yama San Sushi Honten (Omicho) — nigiri + kaisendon, ~¥3-4k pp; Fri Nov 20 lunch
- [ ] Shimada Suisan (Omicho) — snow crab, oysters, uni from the stall; Sat Nov 21 lunch
- [ ] Sushi Ikuta — 8-seat omakase, ~¥18,700 pp; **book ahead**, Sat Nov 21 dinner
- [ ] Unagi Yondaime Kikukawa (near station) — ~¥6k pp; Fri Nov 20 dinner
- [ ] Yakitori Ryo — charcoal yakitori; Sun Nov 22 dinner
- [ ] Kanazawa Curry Laboratory — casual lunch
- [ ] Cafe Tamon — pancakes
- [ ] Takada — fresh mochi in the samurai district
- [ ] Omicho on Sun Nov 22 and Mon Nov 23 (holiday): about half the shops closed, eat there Fri/Sat
