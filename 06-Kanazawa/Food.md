## Suggestions
- [ ] Omicho Market — kaisendon (seafood bowls), grilled crab, sushi; go for lunch
- [ ] Kanazawa is famous for gold-leaf soft-serve ice cream
- [ ] Jibuni (duck/wheat-gluten stew) — local specialty
- [ ] Sushi: Ippei / Mori Mori Sushi (Kanazawa Station) for value
- [ ] Must-try: snow crab (zuwaigani — Nov season opens!), nodoguro, gold-leaf treats
