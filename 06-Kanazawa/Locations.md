- [ ] Higashi Chaya geisha district — teahouses, Kaikaro
- [ ] Nagamachi samurai district (Nomura House)
- [ ] 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art (Leandro Erlich pool)
- [ ] Nishi Chaya + Kazuemachi smaller chaya districts
- [ ] Omicho Market
- [ ] Myoryuji "ninja temple" — phone booking 076-241-0888 (08:30-17:00, English OK), ~40 min tour in Japanese, ¥1,200 cash; winter hours 09:00-16:00; Sun Nov 22 09:30
- [ ] Oyama Shrine — stained-glass gate, near the castle
- [ ] Kenrokuen 08:00-17:00, ¥320; Castle Park 08:00-17:00 free, Hishi Yagura ¥320 cash, last entry 16:00
- [ ] Nomura-ke samurai house 08:30-16:30, ¥550
- [ ] Kaikaro teahouse 10:00-17:00, ¥750, closed Wed
- [ ] 21st Century Museum — exhibition zone closed Mon (Tue Nov 24 this year, holiday shift); open to 20:00 Fri/Sat
- [ ] Tsuzumi-mon gate at Kanazawa Station — free, best after dark
